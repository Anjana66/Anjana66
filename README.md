## About Me

I'm an Information Technology graduate (B.Tech, 2022) currently pursuing an **MCA in Generative AI** at SRM Institute of Science and Technology. My work sits where data analytics meets applied AI: I build Python and Power BI projects that turn financial and business data into risk metrics, forecasts, and dashboards.

Before moving into analytics, I spent about a year at Ascend International working on digital innovation, business workflows, and process improvement, and I worked as a freelance technical content writer for Aumbit (May to November 2025), producing user guides, help documentation, FAQs, and knowledge-base content.

I'm looking for roles as a **Data Analyst, Power BI Developer, Python Developer, Generative AI Developer, AI Prompt Engineer, or AI/ML Analyst**, and I learn best by building complete, end-to-end projects.
